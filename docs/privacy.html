AirSteps Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

AirSteps is a small Home Screen widget that shows the daily step count your Fitbit Air saves into Apple Health. It is built to keep your data on your phone.

What AirSteps reads

  • Your step count from Apple Health, limited to the source you choose (normally Google Health / Fitbit).
  • Nothing else. AirSteps does not read heart rate, sleep, location, contacts, or any other Health category.

Where your data goes

  • Nowhere. AirSteps has no servers, no accounts, and no network access.
  • Your latest step count and your daily goal are stored on your device only, in a private container shared between the app and its widget, so the widget can display them.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or analytics, and is never sold or shared with anyone.

Analytics and tracking

AirSteps contains no analytics, no crash reporting SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party code.

Your control

  • You can withdraw Health access at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Health → Data Access & Devices → AirSteps.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

Children

AirSteps does not knowingly collect any personal information, from children or anyone else.
